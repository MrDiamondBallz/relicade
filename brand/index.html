01

A familiar letter. A new silhouette.

THE CUT-R / ORIGINAL VECTOR

Cut from the past.
Pointed forward.

A solid R, opened at the counter. The diagonal leg gives it direction; the angular cut gives it room to breathe. No controller, cartridge or borrowed nostalgia required.

One shape.

A single editable path. Recognizable without the name.

No effects.

Flat color. Clean edges. The silhouette does the work.

Small. Still Relicade.

Use the symbol for navigation. Use the contained icon for app surfaces.

BARLOW CONDENSED / EDITORIAL DISPLAY

The name has an edge.
The interface stays clear.

The wordmark is drawn, not typeset. Forward-cut terminals and angular shoulders tie every letter back to the symbol.

Display type is condensed and confident. Body copy is Manrope: open, readable, out of the way.

Say what happens. Say what is missing. Give credit to the people doing the work.
